# Delete your Netra account

You can delete your Netra account at any time. There are two ways: in the app,
which is immediate, or by email if you no longer have it installed.

## In the app (immediate)

1. Open **Netra**
2. Tap **Settings** — the gear at the top right
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**
4. Confirm **Delete permanently**

Your sign-in is removed straight away and you are signed out.

## By email

If you have uninstalled Netra or cannot sign in, email
**suchith.arella@gmail.com** from the address you signed up with, with the
subject **Delete my Netra account**.

We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. We may need to ask you to
verify the address if the request does not come from it.

## What gets deleted

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Your account — the email address or Google account you signed in with | **Deleted immediately** on request. This is the only account information we hold. |
| Connection records — your account's anonymous user id and the times it connected | Kept for at most **30 days** in server logs for debugging and abuse prevention, then deleted automatically. Not deleted on request, because they are what would show if someone else had been using your account. |

## What was never ours to delete

**People you memorise never leave your phone.** Their faces are stored only in
Netra's private storage on your device. Delete a person in Settings → People, or
uninstall the app, and they are gone. We have never had a copy.

**Camera and microphone are not recorded.** While a session is running they are
streamed to Google's Gemini API so it can describe what is in front of you, and
they are not stored by us, anywhere. Google's handling is covered by the
[Gemini API Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

**Your settings** — speaking speed, which guidance is on — are on your device
and are removed when you uninstall.

## Deleting everything

Delete your account using either method above, then uninstall Netra. That
removes the account, the memorised people, and your settings. The connection
logs age out on their own within 30 days.
